What is WeIndie?

AI tools and conventions are changing quickly. WeIndie is a small, independent workbench for trying practical ways of working with them—without tying the work to one model, vendor or grand methodology.

Useful on its ownEach piece should solve a recognisable problem without requiring the whole system.
AdaptableThe work belongs in your workflow. You should be able to inspect and change it.
Honest about limitsIf none of the current work fits a problem, the useful answer may simply be “not yet”.

Where this is right now

Early and evolving. The first skills are being tested through real work. More notes, examples, workflows and experiments will be added when they have proved useful.
